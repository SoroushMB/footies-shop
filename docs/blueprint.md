# **App Name**: Apex Football Gear

## Core Features:

- Jersey Display: Showcase of jerseys (club and national teams) with filters.
- Football Showcase: Display match and training footballs.
- Apparel, Footwear, Accessories: Display tracksuits, windbreakers, cleats and goalkeeper gloves
- Featured Product Carousel: Carousel featuring new and best selling items on the home page.
- Bottom Section Replication: Copy of the icon-football.com's bottom section for free shipping, newsletter and payment options.
- Shopping Cart Functionality: A system for customers to add items to their shopping cart.
- Product Suggestion Engine: AI powered tool for suggesting related products to users. AI will reason when or if a given product suggestion is appropriate for a specific customer.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Charcoal Gray (#1A1A1A) for text and dark elements.
- Secondary color: Off-White / Light Gray (#F5F5F5) for content backgrounds.
- Glass elements: White with ~20% opacity (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)).
- Accent color: Vibrant Electric Green (#00FF7F) for call-to-action buttons, active links, and highlights.
- Headings: 'Poppins' (geometric sans-serif, bold or semi-bold) for a modern look.
- Body text: 'Poppins' (geometric sans-serif, regular weight) for readability and consistency.
- Glassmorphism style: Layered content panels with transparency, background blur (backdrop-filter: blur(10px)), and a subtle, 1px light border.
- Background: Large, high-quality, subtly blurred images of stadiums or abstract green pitch patterns.
- Use simple, clean icons for category representation and the replicated bottom section from icon-football.com.
- Subtle animations for hover effects on product cards and button interactions to enhance the premium feel.
